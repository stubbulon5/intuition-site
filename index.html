Common questions

Does Intuition need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline, on every device.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in and no account of any kind.

Why can't I hear anything?

Intuition plays through your device's speaker or headphones. Check the volume, and on iPhone make sure the Ring/Silent switch isn't on silent. The guitar's tone and volume are in Settings › Sound.

Can I use my own tuning?

Yes. Open the tuning menu at the top of the screen and choose Customise to build any tuning, add a capo, or pick one of the 18 built-in tunings, including 7- and 8-string.

Do the games need a microphone?

No. Every game is played by tapping the fretboard on screen.

Where are my projects saved?

On your device. You can export a project from the Project screen with the Share button.

Can I replay the tour?

Yes, from Settings › About.
