Privacy Policy

Intuition for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective 1 November 2026

Intuition collects no data at all. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app works completely offline.

What stays on your device

Your projects, progressions, custom tunings, theme choice, game scores and settings are saved on your device using the system's standard app storage. They never leave your device, and we never see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.
  • We don't use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.
  • We don't connect to the internet. Intuition makes no network requests.
  • We don't use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Files you export

If you export a project, Intuition writes a file to the place you choose, or hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app or service you pick.

Children

Intuition is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions? See Intuition support or email intuition-guitar-app@gmail.com.
